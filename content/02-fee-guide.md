# Private Fee Guide

Effective 1 January 2026. All fees are in pounds sterling and include VAT where applicable. These are our private fees; NHS charges are set nationally and listed separately in our NHS and Private Care document.

Fees are a guide. Your dentist will give you a written treatment plan with exact costs before any work begins, and we will not proceed with treatment you have not agreed to.

## Fee increases

We review our fees each January and give at least one month's notice of any change. If you are partway through an agreed course of treatment when fees change, we honour the price on your original treatment plan.
