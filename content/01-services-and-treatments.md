# Services and Treatments

Ashfield Dental Practice offers general, preventive, restorative and cosmetic dentistry for adults and children. Below is what we provide.

## Preventive care

**Routine examination.** A full check of teeth, gums, bite and soft tissues, including an oral cancer screening. We recommend an examination every six months, though patients with healthy teeth and gums may be placed on a nine or twelve month recall at the dentist's discretion.

**Hygienist appointments.** Scale and polish to remove plaque and tartar, with advice on brushing and interdental cleaning. Most patients see the hygienist every six months. Patients with gum disease may be asked to attend every three or four months.

**X-rays.** Small bitewing X-rays are usually taken every two years to check between teeth. A larger OPG (panoramic) X-ray may be taken when planning extractions, implants or orthodontics.

**Fluoride varnish.** Applied to children's teeth twice a year, and to adults at higher risk of decay.

**Fissure sealants.** Protective coatings applied to the biting surfaces of children's back teeth.

## Restorative treatment

**White fillings.** We place tooth-coloured composite fillings as standard. We do not place new amalgam (silver) fillings, though we will repair or replace existing ones.

**Crowns.** Full coverage restorations in porcelain, porcelain bonded to metal, or gold. Crowns are made by an external laboratory and require two appointments roughly two weeks apart, with a temporary crown fitted in between.

**Bridges.** Fixed replacements for one or more missing teeth, anchored to the adjacent teeth.

**Root canal treatment.** Performed in-house for front teeth and premolars. Complex molar cases, retreatments, and teeth with unusual anatomy are referred to a specialist endodontist.

**Extractions.** Straightforward extractions are carried out at the practice. Surgical extractions, including most impacted wisdom teeth, are referred to an oral surgeon.

**Dentures.** Full and partial dentures in acrylic or chrome cobalt. Repairs and relines are also available.

## Cosmetic treatment

**Teeth whitening.** Home whitening using custom-made trays and professional gel, worn overnight or for a few hours daily over two to three weeks. We do not offer in-chair power whitening.

**Veneers.** Thin porcelain facings bonded to the front of teeth to change shape, colour or alignment.

**Composite bonding.** Tooth-coloured material applied directly to reshape or repair a tooth in a single visit.

## Orthodontics

**Clear aligners.** We provide clear aligner treatment for adults and older teenagers with mild to moderate crowding or spacing. Treatment typically runs six to eighteen months.

We do not provide fixed metal braces. Patients needing fixed appliances, and children requiring NHS orthodontic treatment, are referred to a local orthodontic specialist.

## Implants

We do not place dental implants at Ashfield. Patients interested in implants are referred to a implant specialist, and we provide the restorative follow-up care afterwards.

## Children

We see children of all ages and encourage a first visit as soon as the first teeth appear, so that children get used to the surroundings early. Children's examinations, fluoride varnish and fissure sealants are provided.

## What we do not offer

For clarity, the following are not available at this practice: dental implant placement, fixed orthodontic braces, in-chair power whitening, general anaesthetic, and surgical wisdom tooth removal. Where appropriate we refer to trusted local specialists and continue your routine care with us.
