# Appointments and Practice Policies

## Booking an appointment

You can book by telephone during opening hours, in person at reception, or through the online booking form on our website. Online booking is available for examinations and hygiene appointments only. Treatment appointments, emergency appointments and anything for a child under 16 must be booked by phone so we can allocate the right amount of time.

We are usually able to offer a routine examination within two to three weeks. Hygiene appointments are typically available within a week. Emergency appointments are held open each day — see our New Patients and Emergencies document.

## Appointment reminders

We send a reminder by text message three days before your appointment, and a second reminder the day before. If you would rather be reminded by email, or not at all, tell reception and we will update your record.

Reminders are a courtesy. Not receiving one is not a reason we can accept for a missed appointment, so please make a note of your appointment when you book it.

## Cancellation policy

We ask for **at least 24 hours' notice** if you need to cancel or change an appointment. This lets us offer the time to someone else, often a patient in pain.

- Cancelled with 24 hours' notice or more: no charge.
- Cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice: a late cancellation fee may be charged.
- Missed without any contact (a "failure to attend"): a missed appointment fee may be charged.

The fees are:

| Appointment | Fee |
|---|---|
| Examination or hygiene appointment | £30 |
| Treatment appointment, 30 minutes or less | £45 |
| Treatment appointment, longer than 30 minutes | £75 |

We do not charge for a first occurrence. We apply the fee from the second late cancellation or missed appointment onward, and we will always look at the circumstances first — genuine emergencies and sudden illness are not charged for.

Where a fee has been applied, it must be paid before your next appointment is booked.

Repeated missed appointments may result in us asking you to find another practice. For NHS patients, this follows NHS guidance on removing patients from a practice list.

## Running late

If you arrive more than 10 minutes late for an appointment, we may not be able to see you, because the time remaining is not enough to complete the treatment safely. We will always try to fit you in, but if we cannot, the appointment is treated as a late cancellation.

If we are running late, reception will tell you when you arrive and give you the option to rebook.

## Bringing someone with you

You are welcome to bring a friend or family member. There is seating for one accompanying adult in each surgery.

Children under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian, who needs to remain in the practice for the duration of the appointment. A parent or guardian must give consent for any treatment on a child under 16.

## Chaperones

You may request a chaperone to be present during any appointment, at any time, without giving a reason. Just ask reception or your dentist.

## Nervous patients

Tell us when you book if you are anxious about dental treatment. We will allocate a longer appointment so nothing feels rushed, and you can arrange to visit the practice beforehand simply to meet the team and see the surgery without any treatment taking place. There is no charge for a familiarisation visit.

## Feedback and complaints

We would rather hear about a problem than not. Speak to reception, or ask for our practice manager. Our written complaints procedure is available on request. We acknowledge complaints within three working days and aim to respond fully within 10 working days.

## Your records and privacy

Your dental records are confidential. We share information with other healthcare professionals only where it is necessary for your care, or where we are required to by law. You have the right to request a copy of your records — put the request in writing to the practice manager and we will respond within one month.
