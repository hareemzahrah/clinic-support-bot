# Insurance and Payment

## When payment is due

Payment for routine appointments — examinations, hygiene visits and emergency appointments — is taken on the day.

For courses of treatment costing more than £400, we take a 50% deposit at the first appointment and the balance on completion. For laboratory work such as crowns, bridges and dentures, the deposit covers the laboratory fee and is non-refundable once the work has been sent.

NHS charges are payable at the first appointment of the course of treatment.

## How you can pay

We accept:

- Debit and credit cards, including Visa, Mastercard and American Express
- Contactless and mobile payments
- Bank transfer, by arrangement in advance
- Cash

We do not accept cheques.

## Dental insurance

We are happy to treat patients with dental insurance. We are **not** able to bill insurers directly. You pay us at the time of treatment, we give you an itemised receipt, and you claim the money back from your insurer.

We are familiar with claims from the following insurers and can provide the documentation they typically require:

- Bupa Dental Insurance
- Denplan (see also our own plan below)
- Cigna
- WPA
- Simplyhealth
- AXA Health

If your insurer is not listed, you can still be treated here. Bring any claim form to your appointment and we will complete our section.

Insurance policies vary widely in what they cover, waiting periods before you can claim, and annual limits. We cannot tell you what your policy covers — you will need to ask your insurer directly before treatment if you want certainty.

## Our practice plan

The Ashfield Care Plan is a monthly payment plan run through the practice. For a fixed monthly fee it includes:

| Plan | Monthly fee | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | £18.50 | Two examinations, two hygiene appointments, routine X-rays, 10% off other treatment |
| Complete | £29.00 | Two examinations, four hygiene appointments, routine X-rays, 15% off other treatment |
| Child (under 18) | £9.50 | Two examinations, fluoride varnish, routine X-rays |

The plan is not insurance. It spreads the cost of routine care and gives a discount on everything else. It does not cover accidents, emergencies away from the practice, or treatment started before you joined.

Plan membership requires a Direct Debit. There is a one-month notice period to cancel and no joining fee.

## Payment plans for larger treatment

For treatment over £750 we offer interest-free finance over 10 months through a third-party provider, subject to a credit check. There is no deposit requirement on finance agreements, and no fee if you settle early.

For treatment over £2,000 we can arrange extended finance over 24 to 36 months. Interest applies on these agreements and the rate will be shown on your credit agreement before you sign.

Ask at reception and we will run an illustration showing the monthly cost before you commit to anything.

## Refunds

If you pay a deposit and cancel before any laboratory work has been ordered or any treatment has begun, we refund the deposit in full. Once laboratory work has been ordered, the laboratory portion is non-refundable.

If you are unhappy with treatment you have paid for, speak to us first. Our complaints procedure is on request at reception and we aim to resolve concerns within 10 working days.
