## Bank holidays and closures

We are closed on all English bank holidays. We close between Christmas Eve and New Year's Day inclusive, reopening on 2 January or the next working day. Closure dates are displayed at reception and on our website from November each year.

For urgent dental problems while we are closed, call our usual number and follow the answerphone instructions, or contact NHS 111.

## Getting here

**By car.** Ashfield Road runs between the town centre and the ring road. We are on the left-hand side heading out of town, immediately after the parade of shops.

**Parking.** We have six patient parking spaces at the rear of the building, accessed via the lane beside the pharmacy. Spaces are for patients attending appointments only and are not reserved — if the car park is full there is free on-street parking on Ashfield Road, limited to two hours, and a pay-and-display council car park on Market Street, a four-minute walk away.

**By bus.** The 14 and 22 services stop on Ashfield Road directly outside the practice. The 7 stops on Market Street.

**By train.** Milbury station is approximately fifteen minutes' walk, or a five-minute taxi ride.

**Cycling.** There is a bike rack in the rear car park.

## Accessibility

The practice is on a single storey with step-free access from the rear car park. The front entrance on Ashfield Road has one shallow step; the rear entrance has none and is the better route for wheelchair users and pushchairs.

All three surgeries, the waiting room and the accessible toilet are on the ground floor and reachable without steps. Doorways throughout are wide enough for a standard wheelchair.

If you have access needs we should know about, tell reception when you book and we will make sure the right surgery and enough time are allocated.

## Languages

Reception and clinical staff speak English. If you would find it easier to be seen with an interpreter present, tell us when you book and we will arrange the appointment so that a family member or interpreter can attend with you.

## Facilities

There is a waiting room with seating for twelve, an accessible toilet, baby changing facilities, and free wifi for patients. The network name and password are displayed at reception.

## Regulation

Ashfield Dental Practice is registered with the Care Quality Commission. All our dentists and hygienists are registered with the General Dental Council. Our CQC registration certificate and professional indemnity details are displayed at reception.
